WHY WE STAND TOGETHER

Closing a neighborhood school affects the entire neighborhood.

♥

Children & families

School changes can disrupt friendships, routines, support networks, and the sense of belonging built over years.

⌂

Homes & property values

Access to a neighborhood school is a factor in many families’ housing decisions. A closure could affect buyer interest and home values; the size of any impact requires independent analysis.

✦

Community identity

Sunrise connects current families, alumni, neighbors, local volunteers, and future students across English Hill.

OUR PRIORITIES

We want a complete picture before an irreversible decision.

We support a transparent review of enrollment forecasts, future neighborhood turnover, realistic cost savings, transportation effects, educational continuity, and alternatives to closure.

We also want the district to consider long-term neighborhood growth and how a local school contributes to the community’s vitality.
