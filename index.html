OUR COMMUNITY

Neighbors standing together.

Stand for Sunrise is a community-led effort by English Hill residents and Sunrise supporters. We care about our neighborhood school, our families, and the future of our community.

We are an independent community initiative, not an official PTA or school district organization.

02 / CURRENT ISSUE

Sunrise Elementary faces possible closure.

Northshore School District is considering school closure scenarios that include Sunrise Elementary. We believe the decision should consider the impact on students and families and the wider English Hill community.

⌂

Neighborhood & Homes

A neighborhood school can influence homebuyer interest and community appeal. Any property-value impact deserves careful study.

♥

Community Connections

Sunrise brings families and neighbors together and supports a strong sense of belonging.

✦

Long-Term Future

Enrollment, neighborhood change, and alternatives to closure should be considered before a lasting decision.
