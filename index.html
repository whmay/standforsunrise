WHO WE ARE

Neighbors standing together.

Stand for Sunrise is a community-led effort by English Hill residents and Sunrise supporters. We care about our neighborhood school, our families, and the future of our community.

We are an independent community initiative, not an official PTA or school district organization.

FAQ

Your questions, answered.

What is Stand for Sunrise?

We are an independent, community-led effort by English Hill residents and Sunrise supporters, preparing legal support to help evaluate options for protecting our school and community.

Can I donate right now?

We are not accepting donations yet. Contribution details and applicable terms will be shared when the fund is ready.

What would the fund support?

The planned fund would support research, professional consultation, and evaluation of potential legal options. Final permitted uses will be published before contributions open.

Is a lawsuit or outcome guaranteed?

No. Any legal action would depend on professional advice, available resources, and the organization’s decision-making process. No outcome is guaranteed.

What happens to unused contributions?

The contribution and refund policy is being finalized. Applicable conditions will be published before fundraising opens.

Is Stand for Sunrise affiliated with the PTA or district?

We are independent of, and are not endorsed by, the PTA or Northshore School District.

How can I help or contact you?

Email contact@standforsunrise.org to ask questions or offer volunteer expertise. You can also visit the Facebook community group.
