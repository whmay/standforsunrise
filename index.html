WHO WE ARE

Neighbors standing together.

Stand for Sunrise is a community-led effort by English Hill residents and Sunrise supporters. We believe decisions about our neighborhood school should consider not only students and families, but also the long-term well-being of the entire community.

We are independent of the school district and PTA.

COMMUNITY FUND

Fundraising is in preparation.

We are working toward an independently managed fund to support community advocacy, research, and professional consultation. We are not accepting donations yet. Details will be shared once the fund and its safeguards are ready.
