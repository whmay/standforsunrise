CONTRIBUTE

Contributions are not tax-deductible.

Contributions to Stand for Sunrise are not deductible as charitable contributions for federal income tax purposes.

Conditional returns may be available.

Under certain conditions, unused funds may be returned in accordance with the final Contribution & Return Policy and applicable law. Returns are not guaranteed and may be partial.

Please review the contribution terms before contributing. The final policy will explain eligibility, conditions, how any return is calculated, and applicable exclusions. It will be available before contributions open.
