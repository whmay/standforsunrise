Contribution Terms — Version 1

Defined terms are in Section 2. This page provides contribution terms for review and awareness; no signature is collected here.

1. THE CORPORATION

1.1 Identity. Stand for Sunrise, of [City], Washington, is an independent nonprofit corporation operating under section 501(c)(4) of the Internal Revenue Code. It is not part of any unit of government, including [the school district] and Sunrise Elementary School, and it does not act for them.

1.2 Contact details. Principal office: [street address], [City], WA [ZIP code]. Website: standforsunrise.org. Email for notices to the Treasurer: [email address].

1.3 Registration. [Currently registered with the Washington state secretary of state as required by law. Stand for Sunrise's registration under chapter 19.09 RCW is on file with the Secretary of State, and additional financial and other information is available from the Secretary of State at 1-800-332-4483 or [Secretary of State charities web page].]

2. DEFINITIONS

In these Contributor Terms, "Articles" means the filed articles of incorporation of the Corporation, as amended or restated, and "Bylaws" means the bylaws of the Corporation, as amended or restated. The terms defined below are also defined in the Articles (Articles I and VIII) or the Bylaws (Sections 1.1 and 1.3); for these Contributor Terms, the definitions in this Section 2 govern. Until the amended and restated articles of incorporation adopted by the Board on [date of adoption] are filed, references to Article VIII of the Articles are to Article VIII of those adopted articles.

"Corporation" means Stand for Sunrise, a Washington nonprofit corporation under chapter 24.03A RCW (UBI 606 321 263).

"Board" means the board of directors of the Corporation.

"Director" means a member of the Board.

"Officer" means an officer of the Corporation elected or appointed under Bylaws Article IV.

"Insider" means a Director, an Officer, or any other person in a position to exercise substantial influence over the affairs of the Corporation.

"Relative" of an individual means the individual's spouse or domestic partner, parent, child, sibling, grandparent or grandchild, the spouse or domestic partner of any of them, and any person sharing the individual's household.

"Ledger" means the Master Contributor Ledger kept by the Treasurer.

"Financial Controls Policy" means the written policy, not part of the Bylaws, that the Board adopts by resolution under Bylaws Section 5.5.

"Payment Error" means a duplicate payment, the excess part of a payment made in the wrong amount, or a payment made by unauthorized use of a card or account.

"Contribution" means money or other property given to the Corporation as a gift, other than a Payment Error or a gift the Corporation declines and returns before recording it in the Ledger as accepted.

"Contributor" means the person, individual or entity, who makes a Contribution, as identified in the Ledger.

"Contributor Terms" means the written donation terms approved by Board resolution, each version identified by a label and approval date (the first: "Contributor Terms CT-1, approved by the Board on [date]"), as set out in the Contributor Agreement and on the Corporation's donation page.

"Conditional Contribution" means a Contribution of money made under a version of the Contributor Terms that states the condition described in paragraph 3(b) of Article VIII of the Articles, as Section 7.5 does, which version the Contributor accepted in a signed writing (including an electronic signature) or by click-through acceptance before or at the time of the Contribution.

"Net Contribution" of a Contributor means the money the Corporation actually received from that Contributor as Conditional Contributions made while the Contributor was not an Insider, counted before payment-processing fees, less all amounts refunded, reversed or charged back on them and any part of them set aside under clause (b) of the definition of Remaining Net Assets.

"Total Contributions" means all money the Corporation actually received as Contributions, whether or not Conditional Contributions and including Contributions of Insiders, counted before payment-processing fees, less all amounts refunded, reversed or charged back and any amount set aside under clause (b) of the definition of Remaining Net Assets.

"Remaining Net Assets" means the Corporation's money left after (a) all known liabilities and obligations, including winding-up costs and any reserve the Bylaws require, are paid, satisfied and discharged or adequately provided for, and (b) property that a trust, or a gift instrument other than the Contributor Terms, requires to be returned, transferred or used for a particular purpose is set aside for it. It excludes property received as a Contribution of property other than money, and its proceeds.

"Return Amount" of a Contributor means the Contributor's Net Contribution multiplied by the lesser of one and the quotient of Remaining Net Assets divided by Total Contributions. Any amount later released from a reserve is added to Remaining Net Assets, and any resulting increase is paid. [A Return Amount, or an increase, of less than $[25] is not paid.]

"Charitable Recipient" means [name of charity or public body] or, if at the time of distribution it does not exist, is not an entity or government described in RCW 24.03A.906(2)(b)(i) or (ii), or declines the distribution, one or more entities operated exclusively for charitable purposes, or a federal, tribal, state or local government for a public purpose, as the Board selects in the plan of distribution.

3. PURPOSE AND USE OF FUNDS

3.1 Purpose. Contributions support the Corporation's purposes stated in Article III of the Articles and Bylaws Section 2.1, including its efforts to protect and preserve public education resources and community schools within Northshore School District, promote accountability and legal compliance by public educational institutions, and safeguard the educational interests of students, families, and surrounding communities.. The Corporation does not promise any particular result.

3.2 Use of funds. The Board decides how and when Contributions are used. The Corporation may use them for any lawful activity within its purposes, including (not limited to) legal fees and litigation costs; consultants and other professional services, such as educational, demographic and financial analysis; research; community outreach, communications, advocacy and lobbying; fundraising; insurance; filing, registration and other government fees; accounting, banking and payment-processing costs; indemnification and other payments the Articles or Bylaws require; other administrative costs; and winding-up costs.

3.3 No use restriction or trust. Contributions are not restricted to any particular use, are not held in trust, and may be combined with the Corporation's other money. A narrower restriction applies to a Contribution only if the Board approves it and an authorized Officer agrees to it in a separate signed record. Such a record must state that the Board decides whether, when and how to pursue the activity it names, and that the restriction ends, and the Contribution may be used as Section 3.2 provides, if the Board decides not to pursue that activity or to end it, or completes it with money left over.

3.4 Political activity and pay. Contributions may be used to influence the selection, nomination, election or appointment of any individual to public office, or to support or oppose a ballot proposition, only as Bylaws Section 2.4 permits. Directors serve without pay; documented, reasonable expenses may be reimbursed as Bylaws Section 9.3 provides.

3.5 Board decisions. The Board decides how the Corporation pursues its purposes (Bylaws Section 3.5), including which advocacy, research and other activities to undertake and when, which lawyers, consultants and other professionals to engage, and whether, when and how to start, join, fund, continue, appeal, settle or end any lawsuit, appeal or other legal proceeding, or to pursue none. For a proceeding brought by others to which the Corporation is not a party, the Board decides only whether, and on what terms, the Corporation pays for it; the parties to it and their lawyers control it. The Board may change any such decision, and no Contribution gives a right, other than the approval right in Section 7.8, to require, approve or veto one. Except as Sections 3.3, 3.4, 7.4 and 7.5 provide, the Corporation does not promise to take, or not to take, any particular advocacy or legal action, and no Contribution is conditioned on any action or result. Subject to Sections 3.3 and 7.4, if the Board decides not to pursue, or to end, any activity or legal proceeding, Contributions may be used for any other activity within the Corporation's purposes, as Section 3.2 provides. Spending on any activity, whether or not the Contributor favors it, reduces the amount available for Return Amounts.

4. NO MEMBERSHIP, VOTING RIGHTS OR AGENCY

4.1 No membership or voting rights. A Contribution does not make the Contributor a member of the Corporation. It gives no right to vote, to elect Directors, or to manage or direct the Corporation's activities and affairs, which are managed by or under the direction of the Board. The approval right in Section 7.8 does not make any Contributor a member.

4.2 No agency or fiduciary relationship. These Contributor Terms create no employment, agency, partnership, joint venture or fiduciary relationship between the Contributor and the Corporation, and no Director or Officer acts as a trustee or fiduciary for the Contributor. The Contributor may not bind the Corporation, sign contracts, incur expenses, or solicit or collect money on its behalf unless the Board, or an Officer acting under a Board resolution, authorizes it in writing.

4.3 Volunteers. A Contributor who volunteers acts for the Corporation only within that written authorization and uses only fundraising wording and methods the Board has approved.

4.4 Payments. Checks must be made payable only to "Stand for Sunrise". The Corporation receives Contributions only into accounts in its own name (Bylaws Section 5.2), never into an individual's personal bank, payment-app or crowdfunding account.

5. TAX STATUS

5.1 Not deductible. Contributions or gifts to Stand for Sunrise are not deductible as charitable contributions for federal income tax purposes.

5.2 Status and solicitations. The Corporation's federal tax status is stated in Article IV of the Articles and Bylaws Section 2.2, and Bylaws Section 2.5(c) requires the statement in Section 5.1 in each fundraising solicitation. The Corporation does not give tax advice to Contributors.

6. LIABILITY

6.1 Separate entity. The Corporation is a legal entity separate from its Contributors, Directors, Officers and volunteers, and its debts and obligations are its own. A Contribution does not by itself make the Contributor responsible for any debt, obligation or act of the Corporation.

6.2 Return Amounts. A Contributor who receives a Return Amount may have to give back part or all of it if law requires, for example toward a claim against the Corporation that was not barred (RCW 24.03A.922(2)) or as recoupment of a distribution later held unlawful (RCW 24.03A.610(3)(b)).

6.3 Own acts. These Contributor Terms do not change any person's responsibility for that person's own acts.

7. REFUNDS AND RETURN ON DISSOLUTION

7.1 Gifts. Each Contribution is a gift. It is not a loan, deposit or investment, and it earns no interest. Under these Contributor Terms, the only return of a Contribution is the conditional return on dissolution in Section 7.5. That return is an express condition of each Conditional Contribution, is never more than the Contributor's Net Contribution, and may be small or nothing. These Contributor Terms provide no return of gifts of property other than money.

7.2 Payment Errors. Payment Errors (duplicate payments, overpayments and unauthorized use of a card or account) are not gifts and are returned once confirmed. Contributions are not refundable before dissolution, except as law requires. Disagreement with a decision of the Board, including a decision about legal action, is not by itself a ground for a refund. A confirmed Payment Error is returned through the original payment method where possible. The Contributor should report a suspected Payment Error to the Treasurer within [60] days after the payment, but a confirmed Payment Error is returned whenever it is found. Payment Errors are handled under item 4 of the Financial Controls Policy.

7.3 Acceptance and declined gifts. The Corporation accepts a gift by recording it in the Ledger as accepted, and then sends the Contributor a written receipt. Before then, the Corporation may decline the gift, and it returns a declined gift in full.

7.4 Wind-down. [Option A: Article VIII, paragraph 2 of the Articles, as in effect when these Contributor Terms were approved, requires that:] [Option B: As a term of each Conditional Contribution, the Corporation agrees that:] Within [90] days after the earliest of (a) the later of the school district board's final decision on whether to close or consolidate Sunrise Elementary School and the end of every appeal under RCW 28A.645.010 or other legal proceeding challenging that decision or, if none is filed, the last day on which an appeal under RCW 28A.645.010 could have been filed; (b) [12] consecutive months during which no proposal to close or consolidate the school is pending before the district board; or (c) [date], the Board shall authorize dissolution unless a majority of the Directors in office finds in writing, giving reasons, that continuing is necessary to the Corporation's purposes. A finding under this Section made in good faith is conclusive.]

7.5 Return on dissolution. The Corporation accepts and holds each Conditional Contribution upon the express condition that, if the Corporation dissolves, it will return the Contributor's Return Amount to the Contributor; this condition occurs by reason of the dissolution. [If the Corporation merges into another entity or converts into another type of entity, the condition in this Section also occurs by reason of that transaction (RCW 24.03A.715(3) and 24.03A.770(3)), and Return Amounts are computed as if the Corporation had dissolved on the transaction's effective date.] The Return Amount is paid in money only after all known liabilities and obligations of the Corporation, including winding-up costs, are paid or adequately provided for; only to the extent RCW 24.03A.155, 24.03A.906 and 24.03A.916 permit; and, where RCW 24.03A.908 applies, only after the plan of distribution is approved, or deemed approved, by the Attorney General, or approved by a court, under RCW 24.03A.908(5). The total of all Return Amounts shall not exceed Remaining Net Assets; if it would, each Return Amount is reduced by the same percentage. A Return Amount may be small or nothing. All other remaining property, except property subject to other gift terms, goes to the Charitable Recipient, including any part of a Return Amount that is waived[, below the minimum in the definition of Return Amount] or not permitted by law to be paid. Unclaimed Return Amounts are handled as chapter 63.30 RCW requires. This condition binds the Corporation under these Contributor Terms. A Return Amount is a distribution under RCW 24.03A.906(2)(e) or (3); it is not a liability or obligation of the Corporation for purposes of RCW 24.03A.906(1), this Section or the definition of Remaining Net Assets. The Corporation will not reduce a Return Amount attributable to Conditional Contributions already received without the Contributor's consent in a record, except as required by law, as stated in the written advice of Washington counsel retained by the Corporation, or by a court order or an objection of the Attorney General under RCW 24.03A.908(5).

7.6 Insiders. The Contributor waives any return of a Contribution made while the Contributor is an Insider, and waives any Return Amount if the Contributor is an Insider at any time from [12] months before the Board authorizes dissolution or, for any other dissolution, before it takes effect, until the final distribution; each waived amount goes to the Charitable Recipient. The Contributor will tell the Treasurer in writing within [30] days after becoming an Insider.

7.7 Dissolution process. Dissolution and distribution follow Article VIII, paragraphs 2 to 4 of the Articles and Article VI of the Bylaws (Sections 6.1 to 6.5), which provide for a written plan of distribution, notice to creditors, provision for later claims, notice to the Attorney General where required, and written legal advice before any Return Amount is paid.

7.8 Amendments to the Articles or Bylaws. Under Article VIII, paragraph 5 of the Articles and Bylaws Section 14.2, no amendment to the Articles or Bylaws reduces the Contributor's Return Amount attributable to Conditional Contributions already received without the Contributor's approval in a record, except as those provisions permit.

7.9 Payment of Return Amounts. The Corporation pays each Return Amount to the Contributor at the most recent address the Contributor has given the Treasurer. For a joint Contribution, it pays the joint Contributors together or, if one has died, the survivor. Any amount payable while the Contributor is under 18 is paid to the conservator or guardian of the Contributor's property, if one has been appointed. Otherwise, it is paid, as RCW 11.114.070 permits, to the parent or guardian who signed this form, or another adult member of the Contributor's family, as custodian for the Contributor under the Washington uniform transfers to minors act. The right to a Return Amount may not be assigned except by operation of law.

8. NOTICES AND PRIVACY

8.1 Email notices. The Contributor agrees to receive notices about Contributions and these Contributor Terms, including notices of dissolution and of any Return Amount, by email at the address in Section 10, as updated under Section 8.2. The Corporation may also use the Contributor's mailing address. The Corporation will send the Contributor notice when the Board authorizes dissolution and again before paying any Return Amount.

8.2 Contact updates. The Contributor will tell the Treasurer in writing of any change to the Contributor's email or mailing address. A notice sent to the most recent address the Contributor has given the Treasurer is effective when sent. Notices from the Contributor to the Corporation go to the Treasurer at the email address or principal office in Section 1.2.

8.3 Confidentiality and use. The Corporation takes reasonable measures to keep Contributor information confidential and uses it only to administer Contributions, keep the Ledger, send receipts and notices, comply with law, and make any other use the Contributor consents to in Section 10. Routine access to Contributor contact details is limited to the Officers who need them. Directors may see Contributor information for purposes related to their duties and must keep it confidential (Bylaws Sections 7.2, 13.1 and 13.2); others may see it only where the Bylaws or law give a right of access. Service providers and professional advisers, such as the payment processor, accountants and legal counsel, receive Contributor information only as needed to provide services to the Corporation and must keep it confidential.

8.4 No sale; publication only with consent. The Corporation does not sell, rent or trade Contributor information. It does not publish a Contributor's name or the amount of a Contribution without the Contributor's consent, unless law requires it, such as campaign-finance reporting under Title 29B RCW. It sends campaign updates only to Contributors who ask for them, for example in Section 10.

8.5 Confirmation of records. On written request, the Treasurer will confirm to the Contributor the Contributions recorded in the Ledger for that Contributor. [At least once a year, the Corporation will send Contributors a summary of total Contributions received, spending by category and the remaining balance.]

9. GENERAL TERMS

9.1 Scope. These Contributor Terms apply to the Contribution described in Section 10 and to each later Contribution of money the Contributor makes, unless the Contributor accepts a later version of the Contributor Terms for that Contribution. A Contribution made before the Contributor accepts these Contributor Terms is not a Conditional Contribution.

9.2 Joint and entity Contributors. Two individuals who give jointly are together one Contributor, and both sign. An individual who signs for an entity confirms that he or she is authorized to do so. If either individual giving jointly is an Insider, the joint Contributor is an Insider for these Contributor Terms. An entity in which one or more Insiders, alone or with their Relatives, hold more than 35 percent of the voting power, profits interest or beneficial interest is an Insider for these Contributor Terms. [A Relative of an Insider is treated as an Insider.]

9.3 Minors. If the Contributor is under 18, a parent or legal guardian must also sign. By signing, the parent or guardian consents to the Contribution and agrees to be bound by these Contributor Terms. [The Corporation may decline a gift from a person under 18.]

9.4 Electronic signatures and records. The Contributor and the Corporation agree that this transaction may be conducted by electronic means. An electronic signature, including a typed name or an acceptance box checked on the Corporation's donation page before payment, has the same effect as a handwritten signature. The Corporation keeps a record of each acceptance with its date and time, the Contributor's email address and the payment reference.

9.5 Governing law and venue. These Contributor Terms are governed by the laws of the State of Washington. An action between the Contributor and the Corporation arising out of these Contributor Terms or a Contribution shall be brought only in a court in [King] County, Washington, unless law requires otherwise.

9.6 Entire agreement. Except for a separate record under Section 3.3 and an amendment under Section 9.7, these Contributor Terms are the entire agreement between the Contributor and the Corporation about each Contribution they cover, and the complete gift instrument for it. They control over any conflicting solicitation or other material. Statements outside these Contributor Terms about the Corporation's plans or the prospects of any effort, including statements in a solicitation, are not promises, and those plans may change. If the text of Contributor Terms CT-1 on the donation page differs from this form, the text the Contributor accepted controls for the Contributions made on that acceptance.

9.7 Amendments to these Contributor Terms. These Contributor Terms may be amended for a Contribution already received only in a record signed by the Contributor and by an Officer authorized by Board resolution, or as required by law, as stated in the written advice of Washington counsel retained by the Corporation, or by a court order or an objection of the Attorney General under RCW 24.03A.908(5). A later version of the Contributor Terms applies only to Contributions made after the Contributor accepts it.

9.8 Severability. If any part of these Contributor Terms is held invalid or unenforceable, the rest remains in effect. If the condition in Section 7.5 is held invalid or unenforceable, in whole or in part, each Contribution remains a completed gift, and any amount that cannot be returned goes to the Charitable Recipient.

9.9 No third-party rights. Only the Contributor and the Corporation, and their successors by operation of law, have rights under these Contributor Terms. No student, family, school, community group or other person is a third-party beneficiary of them. This Section does not limit the authority of the Attorney General.
